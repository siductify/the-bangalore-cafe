01 · Our Story

A pure-veg love letter to Bangalore.

We are The Bangalore Cafe — a pure vegetarian restaurant dishing out a mix of authentic and experimental cuisine from across the globe. From the gallis of India to the streets of Italy, our kitchens roam across North Indian, Chinese, Mexican and Italian.

There's an Outhouse for quick bites — chaats and amazing tea and coffee — a Dessert House for every sweet craving, a Drive-In for a feel of old-school Bangalore, and a massive Banquet Hall to host the most important days of your life.

What more could one want?

03 · On the Plate

South to North — and a little further.

One pure-veg kitchen, roaming happily across cuisines.

The South

Crisp ghee-roast dosas, soft idlis, vadas, and slow-brewed filter kaapi.

The North

Dal makhani, paneer butter masala, and naan straight off the tandoor.

The West

Wood-fired pizza, pastas, and continental classics — plus Mexican and Chinese.

☕
